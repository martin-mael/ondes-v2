## The generator's performance budget

#50's acceptance criterion is a week for a 20k-track library in under 10 s. Both `*.bench.test.ts` files in `packages/scheduling` assert it on CPU time and log the wall time.
- **CI is slow:** the CI runner is about 3–4× slower than the owner's Mac, even in CPU time. On the merge commit, both weeks took about 5.3–5.5 s of CPU on CI and about 1.9 s locally.
- **Back-timing cost:** #46 first doubled the time. The fix, in `rules/history.ts` and `rules/pick.ts`:
  - `removeFromHistory` rebuilds the history index in place and logs the assets whose latest play changed (`changes`);
  - the picker's reused rotation order re-places just those assets.

  `quick-pick.test.ts` checks that the reused order matches the full ranking when picks are taken back.
- **Profiling recipe:** a script that imports `generateDays` and `hardStartSnapshot`, run with `node --cpu-prof`, then sum self and inclusive time per function. Compare a hash of the output before and after any change: it must be byte-identical.

## New gotchas

- **The regeneration freeze point:** the generator now takes `frozenUntil` in its snapshot and plans nothing new before it, not even in a gap. Before that, a gap that back-timing left before a kept marker was refilled inside the frozen hour.
- **`resolve.py` bugs:**
  - When a conflict starts inside a doc comment, the second side loses its opening `/**`. Put it back by hand.
  - It sometimes inserts a stray `},\n{` between functions.

  Typecheck after every resolution.
- **zsh:** `$sha:apps/…` expands with the `:a` modifier. Write `"${sha}:path"`.
- **Migrate's misleading message:** `packages/db` migrate always prints "database is up to date", even after applying. Check `drizzle.__drizzle_migrations` or the tables themselves.
- **S3 tests skip silently:** integration tests skip them unless `.env.example` is loaded (`set -a; . ./.env.example; set +a`).
- **Date-dependent web tests:** tests that compare against "now" must pin the clock (`vi.useFakeTimers({ toFake: ['Date'], now: … })`). Two alert tests failed on any day other than their fixtures'.
- **CI's splice job:** it installs native ffmpeg from apt, and the mirror once crawled into the job timeout. The `.deb` packages are now cached weekly (`apt-ffmpeg-ubuntu-24.04-<week>`), with retries and a step timeout.
- **Flaky engine tests:** the engine adapter's island push test failed once in CI with "other side closed" and passed on rerun. Re-run failed jobs with `gh run rerun <id> --failed` before digging.
- **Shared stack and OrbStack:** Docker went down several times during these waves. Restarting OrbStack or running `pnpm dev:up` is the owner's call each time. Ask, then tell the sessions when it's back.
